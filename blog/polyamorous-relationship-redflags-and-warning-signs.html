Polyamorous-Relationship-Redflags-And-Warning-Signs

As someone who has navigated the complexities of polyamory and ethical non-monogamy, I've learned a thing or two about what to watch out for when it comes to red flags in relationships. Polyamory can be incredibly rewarding, but it's not without its challenges and potential pitfalls. In this post, we'll explore some common polyamorous-relationship-redflags-and-warning-signs that you should be aware of.

Gaslighting and Emotional Manipulation

One of the most insidious red flags in any relationship is gaslighting – when someone tries to manipulate or control your perception of reality. In polyamorous relationships, this can manifest as constant criticism of your partners or attempts to make you feel guilty for pursuing other connections. If you find yourself feeling consistently anxious or uncertain about what's real and what's not, it may be time to re-evaluate the relationship.

For example, let's say your primary partner is jealous of a new connection you've made and starts making comments like "You're just playing with fire" or "Don't get too close to them." If this kind of behavior continues despite your reassurances that you value your connection with them, it may be a sign that they're trying to control the narrative.

Lack of Communication and Boundaries

Polyamorous relationships thrive on open communication and clear boundaries. If you find yourself consistently feeling unsure about what's expected of you or what's okay in terms of interactions with other partners, it may be a sign that there's a lack of communication and boundary-setting.

For instance, if your partner doesn't explicitly discuss their needs and desires regarding jealousy, possessiveness, or monogamy, it can lead to misunderstandings and hurt feelings down the line. Be sure to have regular check-ins with all parties involved to ensure everyone is on the same page.
